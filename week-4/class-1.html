Recursive

How Recursive works

Try the best break first. If a piece is still too big, recurse with a weaker break.

\n\n paragraphs→ \n lines→ spaces / words→ characters

Why we use it

Keeps paragraphs together longer than a naive fixed-width cut — better retrieval chunks for PDFs and wiki text.

Overlap

chunk_overlap copies the tail into the next chunk so boundary sentences are not lost.

Splitter

RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter

RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter(
    chunk_size=1000,
    chunk_overlap=200,
)

1000

Enough for an article-ish passage without flooding the prompt.

200

~20% overlap — homework: try 500 / 1500 and compare.

LangChain recommends Recursive as the default generic text splitter.

Dials

top-k and score threshold

k

How many chunks enter the prompt. More context ≠ always better.

threshold

Drop weak cosine hits. Too high → false “not found”.
