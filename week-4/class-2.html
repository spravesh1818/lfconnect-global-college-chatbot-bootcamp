Callback

Wire the chat handler

def respond(message, history, k, score_threshold):
    answer, hits = ask(message, k=k, score_threshold=score_threshold)
    history = history + [
      {"role":"user","content":message},
      {"role":"assistant","content":answer},
    ]
    return history, format_hits(hits)

Control

Slider: top-k (1–8)

Low k

Focused answers; may miss split articles.

High k

More coverage; risk of diluted / noisy context.

Default in notebook: k=4.

Week 4/Class 2Capstone
01 / 01← → · Space · E edit

Control

Slider: score threshold (0.0–0.8)

Too low

Off-topic questions may still retrieve weak chunks → hallucinations.

Too high

Real constitution questions fail closed.

Default: 0.25 — tune on your PDF.
